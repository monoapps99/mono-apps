Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

Data stored: connected account ID, name, username and picture, access tokens, media, and post history. It is stored on the owner's computer. Access tokens are encrypted.

Use: only to connect the account and publish posts its user creates. Media and captions are sent to the platforms chosen for each post.

Sharing: data is not sold or shared with advertisers. There is no tracking in the tool.

Removal: an account can be removed in the tool at any time. Access can also be revoked in the settings of each platform.

Contact: message @mono.apps on Instagram.
